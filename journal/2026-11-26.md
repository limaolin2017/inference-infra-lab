| Q2b | Where does C go? | "waiting list" → asked front or back → "the front" | **Right (front with a front/back prompt)**. Method `prepend_request` taught again |
| Q3 | Twist: `running` = B, C, A (A came last). A needs a block, none free. Who is kicked out? | "那我就不知道了" | **Miss** → explained: A itself (always the last in `running`, even if it is the one asking) |
| Q3 (restate) | — | "If A came last and needs more blocks, it kicks itself out." | **Right (own words, guided)**. Analogy: last person on a full bus gets off and waits for the next bus |

**Key point (own words):** preemption only happens when a request **already running** needs one more block and none are free. The Scheduler kicks out the **last** one in `running` (can be the requester itself); it goes to the **front** of `waiting` (`prepend_request`).

## Teaching feedback

- **Give the full setup first.** Questions that start mid-scenario ("A needs a block…" with no A introduced) confused the learner 6 times. Rule for reviews: state the premise (what is fixed, who is running, in what order, what is needed) **before** the question.
- Analogies used: parking guard vs boss; full car park; restaurant seats; last person off a full bus; GPU as a box (weights + KV).

## English

- kick out = 踢出去 · prepend = 放到最前面 · FCFS = First Come, First Served (先来先服务)
- Word fix: QV cache → **KV cache**
- Sentences: "What's your question?" · "Explain the setup first." · "Then I don't know." · "The space gets bigger."
