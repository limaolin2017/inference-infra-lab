## Key corrections

- **Compute vs read:** prefill **computes** K/V (compute-bound, TTFT); decode **reads** K/V (memory-bound, TPOT). 先算后读.
- **Two side-by-side causes** (linked as cause → effect 3× now): step time = others' prefill compute (the knob controls this) **+** own K/V read (grows anyway, not the knob).
- **Chunked prefill:** the **last** chunk's step outputs the first token.
- **Lowering the knob** doesn't give decode "more compute" — it makes each step shorter.
- **Preempt frees blocks** → that's why the request must recompute.
