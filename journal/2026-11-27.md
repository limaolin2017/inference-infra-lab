## Part 3 — Compute-bound vs memory-bound

| # | Question | Answer | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q5 | Prefill (2,000-token prompt in one go) vs decode (1 token per step): which is compute-bound, which memory-bound, and why? | "算力不够 = prefill; 搬数据太慢 = decode. Prefill 把大量 prompt 塞到显存里; decode 搬权重一次，不停搬 KV cache" | Direction: **Right, first try, no hint** (swapped 11-23, 11-24). Decode reason: **Partial** (weights moved **every step**, not once). Recalled the chef analogy: cutting board = SRAM, stored on the side = HBM; fixed: the **hands** compute, not the board |
| Q5b | In prefill, why are the chef's hands the limit, not the walk to the fridge? | "在 prefill 阶段，它不涉及搬数据啊，我记得" | **Miss** — both phases move data; "bound" = which takes longer (moving or math), not yes / no |
| Q5c (restate) | — | "prefill 也需要搬数据，把权重搬过来，但只搬一次; decode 要不停的搬 KV cache, 对吧?" | **Mostly right (guided, confidence check)**. Missing: prefill then **computes many tokens** (the actual reason); decode moves **the weights every step too**, not only K/V |
