## Differences from vLLM V1 (do not mix up)

- nano-vllm runs a step as **prefill-only or decode-only**. vLLM V1 puts a prefill chunk and other requests' decode tokens in **the same step**.
- Chunked prefill in nano-vllm is only allowed for the first request in a step.
